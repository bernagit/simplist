## What is Simplist?

Simplist is a lightweight task and list manager built around a simple philosophy:

- 📋 Keep your lists organized
- ✅ Focus on your tasks
- 🤝 Share lists with others
- ⚡ Navigate quickly with keyboard shortcuts
- 🐳 Own your data by self-hosting

No unnecessary complexity—just a fast, modern application that stays out of your way.
