## Calendar

The **Calendar** brings together the dated tasks of your lists in one place.

- **Views**: Day, Week and Month. On a phone it opens on the Day view, which is easier to read.
- **Navigation**: the arrows move to the previous or next period, **Today** goes back to the current date.
- **Agenda**: shows the period as a list, with each task's time, priority and list.
- **Tasks without a time**: shown in the "All day" row.
- **Tasks with a time**: take up the space of their duration (half an hour if none is set).
- **Recurring tasks**: appear on every date they repeat on, in later months too.
- **Colors**: each task has the color of its list.

Tap a task to open its details. You can edit it there, and the path at the top takes you to its list or section.

## Search

Open search with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac), or with **Search** in the sidebar.

As you type it finds:

- **lists**, by name and description;
- **sections**;
- **tasks**, by title and description, also in lists shared with you. Open tasks come first; for each you see its list, section and due date.

Picking a task opens its list, scrolls to the right section and shows the task's details. The back gesture closes the details and leaves you in the list.

With an empty search you get shortcuts to move around: Home, Calendar, My Lists, Shared With Me, New list.
