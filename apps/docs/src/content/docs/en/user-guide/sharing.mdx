## Sharing a list

Only the owner can share a list. Open the list and use **Share** at the top (or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>), or **Share** in the card's menu.

Enter the email of someone who already has an account on this Simplist instance and pick a role:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Editor** | Add, edit, complete and delete tasks and sections |
| **Viewer** | See the list and its tasks, without changing them |
| **Owner** | Everything, including editing, sharing, archiving and deleting the list |

In the same window you see the members, and can **change each one's role** or **remove** them. At the top of the list, avatars show who is in it.

<Aside>
If the person doesn't have an account yet, invite them to Simplist first: see [Invites](#invites) below.
</Aside>

## Lists shared with you

Lists other people shared with you are in **Shared With Me**, with the owner's name and the task progress. The role they gave you decides what you can do.

If the owner archives a shared list, you no longer see it until they restore it.

## Leaving a shared list

To stop seeing a list shared with you, use **Leave list**:

- from the card's **…** menu in **Shared With Me**;
- or from the **…** menu at the top, inside the list.

After you confirm, the list disappears and you stop getting its reminders. To get back in, the owner has to share it with you again.

## Invites

You can only sign up to Simplist with an invite. To invite someone, go to **Settings → Invites** and create a **New invite**:

- you can enter the person's email, if you like;
- you get a **link** to copy and a **QR code** to scan;
- the invite is valid for **7 days** and can be used once.

The list shows each invite's status (pending, used, expired) and lets you **revoke** the unused ones. The instance administrator can limit how many invites each user can create.
