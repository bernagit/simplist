Simplist is built on three things:

- **lists**, which collect the tasks of one area (home, work, groceries…);
- **sections**, which split a list into groups (for example "To do", "Waiting");
- **tasks**, with a date, time, repeat, reminder and priority.

Around them are the **calendar**, **search**, **sharing** with other people and **notifications** on your phone and computer.

If you haven't yet, [install the app](/docs/en/getting-started/installation/) on your device: it works better, especially on a phone.
