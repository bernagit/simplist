## Your lists

**My Lists** shows every list you created. For each one, the card shows:

- how many tasks are still open;
- a progress bar;
- whether the list is shared with other people;
- when it was last updated.

At the top you have:

- **search** by name or description;
- **sorting**: recently updated, name, newest, most open tasks;
- the **Active / Archived / All** filter.

The filter and sorting are remembered.

Lists other people shared with you are in **Shared With Me**: see [Sharing](/docs/en/user-guide/sharing/).

## Creating a list

Use any of these:

- the **New list** button (bottom right on a phone);
- the **+** next to "My Lists" in the sidebar;
- the <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> shortcut;
- search: type "create".

A list has a **title**, an optional **description** and a **color**, which tells it apart in the sidebar, on the cards and in the calendar.

## Editing, archiving and deleting

From a card's **…** menu (or with a right-click or a long press) you can:

- **Edit**: title, description and color. Inside the list also with <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>; the title can also be renamed with a click.
- **Share**: see [Sharing](/docs/en/user-guide/sharing/).
- **More Info**: open and done tasks, sections, members with their role, creation and update dates.
- **Archive**: the list leaves the sidebar and, if it's shared, the other members' lists too, but it isn't deleted. Find it with the **Archived** filter, where you can restore it.
- **Delete**: removes the list with all its sections and tasks.

<Aside type="tip">
To prevent mistakes, Simplist asks for confirmation before archiving or deleting a list. You can turn this off in **Settings → General**.
</Aside>

## Sections

New tasks that aren't in a section sit at the top of the list. When the list grows, group them into sections with **Add Section**, at the bottom of the page.

For each section you can:

- **collapse or expand** it with the arrow next to its title;
- see at a glance **how many tasks** it has and how many are done, in the ring next to its title;
- **rename** it from the **…** menu (on a computer also by clicking its title);
- **delete** it from the **…** menu, together with its tasks;
- **move** it by dragging its handle on the left (⋮⋮).

Only the owner and editors can change sections and tasks. Viewers see the list read-only.
